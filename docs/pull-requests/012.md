## What changes

- **In line, staged, no bypass relay** (`DECISIONS.md` D13, operator decision). The controller sits in the middle of each valve cable. The RS-485 pair (pins 3/4) is split at the controller, and pins 2 (ground) and 5 (+12.7 V, which the valve supplies) pass straight through. Each valve goes through three stages: **pass-through** (the K-99695 stays master and the controller copies every byte), **take-over** (the controller drives the valve and answers the K-99695 for it, so the K-99693 keeps working) and **cut-over** (new panel in, K-99695 and K-99693 powered down). Each segment always has exactly one master. The case for leaving out a bypass relay is the measured result that the valve shuts the water off about 2 s after it stops hearing from its master.
- **One W5500-EVB-Pico2** (RP2350 with W5500 wired Ethernet) running Rust firmware on Embassy, replacing the Pi 4 (D14). In line needs five serial ports: two per valve and one for the panel. The ESP32 lost because its only `no_std` Ethernet driver is `ph-esp32-mac` 0.1.x and its Xtensa cores need the `espup` toolchain fork.
- **TI THVD1410 per port**, with an external 4.7 kΩ pull-down on each `DE` (RP2350 erratum E9) so a resetting board can't drive a valve bus (D15). The THVD1406 auto-direction part I first recommended in the thread is recorded as a wrong turn. Its datasheet says it drives a mark for only 4–14 µs and then leaves the line to the bus bias, while the K-99695 drives every bit.
- **A new wall panel** on its own board, wired over the K-99693's in-wall cable, with RS-485 on one pair and passive DC from the panel's own adapter on the spare pairs. It must work with the internet down (D16, operator decision). This amends D12's scope.
- D1, D2 and D3 are marked superseded with their records left in place. W5 records revision A (never built) and points at `e459eb0`.
- `HARDWARE.md` revision B keeps every section number and adds §16 (panel link). `SHOPPING-LIST.md` and `BUILD-ORDER.md` are rewritten. `DESIGN.md` gets the new safety rules (rule 1 is now one master per segment, with the old wording kept as superseded), firmware sections and phases (3a pass-through, 3b one-valve take-over, 7 cut-over). The orientation docs (`AGENT.md`, both `CLAUDE.md`s, the controller README) and the story log are updated to match.
