_Requested by **Nate**_

The factory K-99695's Saturn valve buses, captured on a Rigol DHO804 at both valve connectors. It covers valve 2 idle and through a start and stop, then valve 1 through a controller restart and two starts and stops. The raw data and the tools that took it are committed so anyone can use them.

## What's in it

- **`research/diagnostics/2026-10-24-saturn-valve-bus/`:** the captures and meter readings. Its `README.md` gives the setup, the measurements, the decoded frames and how to read the files.
  - **Valve 2, at the top level and in `shower-run/`:** triggered polls, edge detail, roll records, and the shower run with its controller calls.
  - **Valve 1, in `valve-1/`:** about three minutes of roll records covering the controller's start-up, plus `run-valve-1/` and `run-valve-2/`.
  - **Per capture:** samples exactly as the scope returned them, metadata with SHA-256, a screenshot and a decoded `.frames.txt`.
  - **Session:** meter readings for both connectors, the scope command log and the session screenshots.
- **`research/tools/saturn-capture/`:**
  - `dho.py` drives the scope: commands, captures, screenshots.
  - `saturn_decode.py` decodes Saturn frames and checks each checksum both ways.
  - `kohler_once.py` makes one allowlisted controller request at a time, 5 s apart.
  - `shower_run.py` runs a start/record/stop/record sequence.
- **`PROTOCOL.md`:** a measured note under "Starting the shower" (see the last finding).
- **`STORY-LOG.md`:** three entries.

## Findings

- **Connector and polarity:** six positions, wired on 2–5. Pin 2 is ground, pins 3 and 4 are the RS-485 pair with pin 4 positive, pin 5 is +12.7 V. This closes HARDWARE.md §15 item 3 (polarity), item 4 (termination and bias), and the pin-count part of item 2.
- **Termination:** 55.6 Ω across the pair with both ends connected, 141.2 Ω at the valve alone. Both valves match within 3%.
- **Timing:** 9600 8N1, cycle 525 ms.
- **Checksum:** all 834 frames, on both buses, check with the sync bytes included in the sum, and none without. `kdtv-proto` CHK-01 excludes them, so it computes one less than the bus carries on every frame. **This PR does not change that code.** The fix is #8, which should merge after this one.
- **Two command sets on the same framing:**
  - **Valve 2:** polled with `07`, commanded with `87`. The command carries mode, setpoint in tenths of a °C, and flow.
  - **Valve 1:** stays at address `00`. It's polled with `01` and `02` and commanded with `81` (mode) and `A4` (outlet bitmask).
- **Start-up:** after a restart, valve 1's bus gets four `3A` address-enquiry broadcasts, then one-time reads `54`, `40`, `16`, `0B`, `0C` and `15`, then steady polling. This happens two minutes before the controller is back on the network.
- **Re-addressing:** at 18:39, with no input, valve 2's poll address changed `00` → `13` and the `3A` broadcasts on its bus began repeating. Why is still open. This may relate to I3.
- **A valve 2 start also starts valve 1:** `quick_shower.cgi` with `valve_num=2` and an empty `valve1_outlet` sends valve 1 a run command and outlet position 1. A valve-1-only start leaves valve 2 off. Position 1 was both valve 1's previous selection and possibly its default, so which rule picks the outlet is unresolved.

## Verified against hardware

- Every frame was decoded from the committed files with `saturn_decode.py`.
- The tools were run against the scope and the controller. `shower_run.py` made the two valve-1 runs.
- Controller contact was fifteen serialised requests, at least 5 s apart, all at 2/5 or below. Nobody was in the shower, at the operator's say-so.

## Not verified

- **The meaning of valve 1's `0x0126`:** it's constant in commands and status.
- **The cause of the re-addressing.**
- **Whether a valve 2 start gives valve 1 its last outlet or its default.**
