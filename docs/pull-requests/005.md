## Salvage from the retired Phase-1 plan

That directory also held a `REPLACEMENT-CONTROLLER.md` written after the move, against this repository's older document set. Every architectural choice in it that this repository also considered, this repository decided differently on measured evidence — the SC16IS752 HAT's shared isolation barrier, Pi 4 over Pi 5, three links over two, bidirectional adapters barred from capture. It is discarded. Four things are carried over, with the product data re-checked rather than trusted:

- **Group F, new** — the two firmware-extraction probes, gated on the board photograph the extraction docs already require. The prices are why it is worth stating: the original had the P&E probe at ~$130 when the part supporting ColdFire V2/V3/V4 is `USB-ML-UNIVERSAL` at **$299**, listed a 26-pin ribbon that ships in that box, and named the FX variant, which is $599 and buys speed only. J904 first — a $9.95 cable answers whether there is a console at all.
- **Group D, the capture tap** — four rules that are properties of bridging a live, already-terminated bus. Strapping `DE` stops the transmitter; only strapping `RE` makes the part a listener. The tap adds neither termination nor bias — two separate omissions, and the bias one is easier to leave populated by accident. The stub stays inches. `PH1-02` gains the `RE` strap; `PH1-06` is new for the loading rules.
- **A priced analyzer candidate** — the commodity FX2 part, named explicitly as *not* meeting the group's isolation requirement. It does satisfy the hardware timestamping a USB-serial front end cannot, and no sample rate substitutes for isolation. Naming what it costs and what it fails beats leaving the group with no number in it.
- **Two bench instruments** — a current-limited supply, which `HARDWARE-SPEC.md` § 12 needs to close the DTV+ 12 V pin-4 measurement and which nothing on the list supplied, and a soldering iron, which the MAX31865 boards require.

Three of three prices re-checked from the old plan were wrong, which is the short argument for discarding it rather than importing it.
